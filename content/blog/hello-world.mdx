## Why this exists

Posts are `.mdx` files in `content/blog/`. The filename becomes the URL slug, so
this file is served at `/blog/hello-world`.

## Frontmatter

`title`, `summary` and `date` are required. `tags`, `updated` and `draft` are
optional. Anything that fails validation stops the build with the offending
filename and field.

## Headings get anchors

Every `##`/`###` heading is given an id derived from its text, so this section is
linkable at `#headings-get-anchors`.
